🚲 Bike Buyers Analytics – Excel Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to understand which customer groups are more likely to purchase a bike based on age, gender, income, region, and commute distance.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created using PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and calculated metrics to present the findings in an easy-to-understand format.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall bike purchase performance
Identify the most active customer age groups
Compare bike purchases between male and female customers
Analyze purchasing behavior by income group
Compare purchases across different regions
Understand the relationship between commute distance and bike purchases
Create an interactive and professional Excel dashboard

📈 Key Findings & Observations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. Overall Purchase Performance

The dataset contains 1,000 customers, of which 481 customers purchased a bike.

This represents a 48% bike purchase rate, meaning almost half of the customers in the dataset purchased a bike.

2. Age Group

The Middle Age group has the highest number of bike purchasers with 230 purchases, followed by the Adult group with 214 purchases.

The Older Adult group has significantly fewer purchases at 37.

Observation:
Bike purchases are concentrated mainly among Middle Age and Adult customers.

3. Gender

Bike purchases are almost evenly distributed:

Male: 242
Female: 239

Observation:
There is very little difference between male and female bike purchases, suggesting that gender does not create a major difference in purchasing volume within this dataset.

4. Income Group

Customers earning 60K–79K have the highest number of purchases at 147.

Other major groups include:

Under 40K: 113
40K–59K: 110
80K–99K: 60
100K+: 51

Observation:
The 60K–79K income group represents the strongest purchasing segment in this dataset.

5. Region

North America has the highest number of bike purchases with 220, followed by:

Europe: 148
Pacific: 113

Observation:
North America is the strongest regional market and could be a priority market for future marketing campaigns.

6. Commute Distance

Customers with a commute distance of 0–1 mile have the highest number of purchases at 200.

Purchases then decrease considerably across longer commute categories:

0–1 Miles: 200
1–2 Miles: 77
2–5 Miles: 95
5–10 Miles: 76
More than 10 Miles: 33

Observation:
Customers with shorter commute distances account for the largest share of bike purchases. This may indicate that bikes are particularly attractive for short-distance commuting.

💡 Business Insights

From the analysis, several potential business opportunities can be identified:

Focus on Middle Age and Adult customers as the primary target segments.
Prioritize customers with 60K–79K income, as this group has the highest purchase volume.
North America should be considered a key market due to its higher number of purchases.
Marketing campaigns could emphasize short-distance commuting and everyday transportation.
Since male and female purchases are almost equal, marketing strategies can target both genders rather than focusing heavily on one group.
